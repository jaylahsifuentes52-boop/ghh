YOUWATCH — QUICK START

1. Extract the youwatch.zip file.
2. Open the "youwatch" folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in your browser.

FEATURES
- Import a YouTube video by pasting its URL.
- Upload a video file from your device.
- Watch YouTube videos on YouTube, or play uploaded videos in a new tab.
- Remove videos from your collection.

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end website. Uploaded video files are kept temporarily in the browser tab and are not uploaded to a server. If you close or refresh the page, locally uploaded videos are cleared. YouTube links are not saved permanently either. To make accounts, cloud uploads, and permanent saved libraries, the site would need a backend and video storage.
